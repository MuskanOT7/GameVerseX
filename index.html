    BAFTA Games Awards 2025 Highlights

    The BAFTA Games Awards 2025 celebrated the most influential and outstanding games of the year.

    This year's awards saw strong representation from British studios, with Senua's Saga: Hellblade II and Fable Reborn receiving multiple nominations. In a public vote, Shenmue (1999) was named the most influential game for its pioneering open-world elements. This year's awards saw strong representation from British studios, with Senua's Saga: Hellblade II leading with 11 nominations. Other notable titles included Still Wakes the Deep and Astro Bot, each receiving eight nominations. Astro Bot was favored for Best Game, though Balatro and Thank Goodness You're Here! were also strong contenders. Melina Juergens was tipped to win Best Performer for her portrayal in Hellblade II.

    A Minecraft Movie Easter Eggs and Tributes

    A Minecraft Movie, which debuted on April 5, 2025, has become a massive box office success, grossing $301 million globally.

    The film, starring Jack Black as Steve alongside Jason Momoa, Danielle Brooks, Sebastian Hansen, and Emma Myers, pays homage to the beloved Minecraft universe through several cleverly hidden Easter eggs. Notably, there's a tribute to Technoblade, a cherished Minecraft player who passed away in 2022, through the image of a crown-wearing pig.

    South of Midnight

    South of Midnight, a Southern Gothic-inspired action-adventure game, is set to launch on Xbox Game Pass on Tuesday, April 8, 2025, at 12 p.m.

    However, only subscribers of the Xbox Game Pass Ultimate and Xbox Game Pass PC tiers will have access to it at launch; it will not be available to Xbox Game Pass Standard or Core subscribers initially. Players who preordered the premium edition of the game, which costs $49.99, received early access beginning April 3.
